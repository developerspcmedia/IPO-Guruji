Privacy Policy for IPO Guruji

This Privacy Policy explains how IPO Guruji handles information when you use our Android application.

Application Name IPO Guruji
Android Package ID com.aistudio.ipopulse.mktqrz
Effective Date November 20, 2026
Developer Contact developers.pcmedia@gmail.com

1. Independent Informational Utility & Disclaimer

IPO Guruji is an independent educational and informational tracker for Indian Mainboard and SME IPOs, including IPO schedules, price bands, live subscription figures, company financials, IPO news, and unofficial Grey Market Premium (GMP) estimates.

IPO Guruji is not registered with SEBI as an investment advisor or research analyst, does not offer brokerage, order execution, or lending services, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEBI, NSE, BSE, or any IPO registrar.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

IPO Guruji does not require user account registration or sign-in, and we do not operate backend servers that collect or store your personal profile.

  • App Preferences: Theme mode, watchlist bookmarks, compact card density, and local alert preferences are stored strictly on your device using Android DataStore.
  • Offline Market Cache: Publicly available IPO schedules, subscription snapshots, and GMP data are cached locally on your device using Android Room for fast loading and offline viewing.

3. Private PAN Profiles & Allotment Status Checks

IPO Guruji provides an optional Allotment Alerts feature where you may choose to save PAN profiles to check IPO allotment status conveniently.

  • Strictly On-Device Storage: Saved PAN profiles and allotment tracking records are stored solely in your device's local database and are explicitly excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. They are never uploaded to or stored on IPO Guruji servers.
  • Direct HTTPS Registrar Queries: When you initiate an allotment check (or enable local allotment status monitoring), your PAN is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS directly from your device to the relevant official SEBI-registered registrar (such as MUFG Intime, KFinTech, Bigshare, or Cameo) solely to retrieve your allotment result.
  • Full User Control: You can mask, reveal, edit, or permanently delete any saved PAN profile inside the Allotment Alerts screen at any time.

4. Biometric & Device Vault Lock

If you enable the optional Vault Lock for Allotment Alerts, IPO Guruji uses Android's built-in system authentication API (USE_BIOMETRIC) to verify your fingerprint, face, or device PIN locally on your device. IPO Guruji never accesses, reads, transmits, or stores biometric templates or device passcodes.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep IPO Guruji free to use, the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (including Banner, Native, Interstitial, and App Open ad formats).

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain data encrypted in transit—such as the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address / approximate location, device identifiers, app interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics—to deliver, personalize, and measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • Review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Opt-Out / Reset Advertising ID: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime on your Android device via Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Permissions Used by the App

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to fetch public IPO data, live subscription feeds, GMP updates, IPO news, registrar checks, and Google AdMob advertisements over HTTPS.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+): Used only if granted by you to send local on-device alerts for new IPO launches, subscription openings, allotment updates, and IPO news.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC (Optional): Used only if you enable Vault Lock to protect the Allotment Alerts section locally on your phone.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob for ad serving, frequency capping, and ad performance measurement.

7. External Links & Public Data Sources

IPO Guruji displays links to third-party websites such as official IPO registrar portals, stock exchanges (NSE/BSE), and financial news outlets. When you tap an external link, you leave IPO Guruji and are subject to the privacy policies and terms of those external websites.

8. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

All network communication performed by IPO Guruji uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Because we do not maintain user accounts or cloud databases storing your personal information:

  • To delete individual PAN profiles or allotment watches, open Allotment Alerts inside the app and delete the desired entry.
  • To permanently remove all locally stored app data and preferences, clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → IPO Guruji → Storage or uninstall the app.

9. Children's Privacy

IPO Guruji provides financial market tracking information intended for an adult audience (18+) and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service integrated into the app, please contact us so we can assist promptly.
