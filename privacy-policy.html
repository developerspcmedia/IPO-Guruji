Privacy Policy — IPO Guruji

Package: com.aistudio.ipopulse.mktqrz • Last Updated: November 2026

Privacy at a Glance

  • Guest browsing requires no sign-in. Use IPO Guruji freely without creating an account.
  • Optional Google Sign-In syncs only your Investor Profile, watchlist bookmarks, and theme/alert preferences privately to your own Google Cloud Firestore account document.
  • PAN profiles stay 100% on-device and are never uploaded to Firebase, Cloud Firestore, or Android cloud backup.
  • Easy account & data deletion anytime directly inside the app or by email.

1. Optional Google Sign-In, Investor Profile & Cloud Sync

You can browse IPO Guruji freely as a guest without creating an account. If you choose to sign in with Google (powered by Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), we receive your Google display name, email address, unique account ID, and profile photo URL.

Your optional Investor Profile—display name, investor category (Retail, S-HNI, B-HNI, or Shareholder), personal investor note, saved IPO watchlist, and theme/notification preferences—is stored in a private Google Cloud Firestore document isolated strictly to your account. When you sign in, any local watchlist bookmarks on your device are merged with your cloud profile. No other user can view, list, or edit your profile, and we never sell or share your personal account information with third-party data brokers.

2. Private PAN Profiles & Allotment Checks

Saved PAN profiles inside Allotment Alerts are stored strictly on your device and excluded from Android cloud backup—they are NEVER uploaded to Firebase, Cloud Firestore, or IPO Guruji servers, even when you are signed in with Google. When you initiate an allotment status check, your PAN is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS directly to official SEBI-registered registrars (such as MUFG Intime, KFinTech, Bigshare, or Cameo) solely to retrieve your allotment result. You can mask, edit, or delete saved PAN profiles inside the app at any time.

3. Biometric & Device Vault Lock

The optional Vault Lock (USE_BIOMETRIC) uses Android's built-in system biometric or device PIN prompt locally on your phone to protect your Allotment Alerts tab. IPO Guruji never accesses, reads, or stores biometric templates or device passcodes.

4. Market Data & Independent Status

IPO Guruji is an independent informational utility and is not affiliated with SEBI, NSE, BSE, SEC, NASDAQ, or any IPO registrar. The app fetches publicly available India Mainboard/SME and USA IPO schedules, live subscription feeds, SEC/exchange filings, news, unofficial grey market (GMP) estimates, and read-only cloud catalog corrections over encrypted HTTPS. Opening external registrar, exchange, filing, or news links takes you to third-party websites governed by their own privacy policies.

5. On-Device Storage, Watchlists & Home Widgets

When browsing as a guest or offline, your theme preference, compact card setting, watchlist bookmarks, Day-3 reminders, GMP watch rules, and cached IPO snapshots are stored locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore. If you add the IPO Guruji Live Radar widget to your home screen, it reads only public IPO summaries cached on your device.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

IPO Guruji displays advertisements via Google AdMob (Banner, Native, Interstitial, and App Open ads) to keep the tracker free to use. Google Mobile Ads may collect device signals, approximate location, app interaction events, and Advertising ID in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Notifications & Background Sync Permissions

Notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+) is completely optional and used solely for IPO alerts covering new IPOs, subscription openings, Day-3 closing reminders, GMP watch thresholds, private allotment updates, and India/USA IPO news. Network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) enable live market refreshes and lightweight background sync jobs for alerts and home screen widgets.

8. Data Security, Retention & Children's Privacy

All network and cloud synchronization traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS, and Cloud Firestore security rules strictly enforce per-user authentication and account isolation. Cloud Investor Profile data is retained only while you maintain an active profile; local data remains on your device until cleared or uninstalled. IPO Guruji is a financial market utility intended for adults (18+) and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

9. Account & Data Deletion Instructions

You can delete your IPO Guruji account and cloud data at any time using either method below:

  • Option A — Instant In-App Deletion: Open IPO Guruji → open the navigation drawer → tap Open Profile (User Profile) → scroll to Account & Cloud Data → tap Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm. Your cloud Investor Profile, synced watchlist, and preferences are permanently deleted immediately.
  • Option B — Web / Email Deletion Request: Email developers.pcmedia@gmail.com from your signed-in Google email address with the subject "IPO Guruji Account & Data Deletion Request". We permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account and Cloud Firestore profile document within 7 business days and confirm by email.
  • Local On-Device Data: Delete saved PAN profiles directly inside Allotment Alerts, or clear app storage / uninstall IPO Guruji to erase all on-device data.

Data Deleted: Google account ID linkage, display name, email address, investor category, investor note, synced watchlist bookmarks, and synced preferences. No personal data is retained after deletion.
